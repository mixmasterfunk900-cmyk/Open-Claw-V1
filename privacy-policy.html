Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how OpenClaw TikTok Tools handles information when used for TikTok-related publishing, automation, or account-management workflows.

Overview

OpenClaw TikTok Tools is used to help the operator connect to TikTok, prepare media, publish or manage content, and review TikTok-related account or content information. At present, the tool is intended for internal/operator use rather than as a public consumer service.

Information We May Process

Depending on the TikTok permissions granted by the operator, the tool may process:

  • TikTok account identifiers, profile information, and public account details.
  • Uploaded or selected videos, images, captions, titles, tags, and related publishing metadata.
  • Content status, performance, analytics, or other TikTok API data made available through authorized access.
  • Authentication tokens or authorization data needed to connect to TikTok APIs.
  • Operational logs needed to diagnose errors, improve reliability, and confirm publishing actions.

How Information Is Used

Information is used only to:

  • Connect authorized TikTok accounts.
  • Prepare, schedule, publish, or manage TikTok content.
  • Display account, media, or analytics information requested by the operator.
  • Debug failures, maintain security, and improve the tool.
  • Comply with applicable platform rules, API requirements, or legal obligations.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information is not shared with advertisers or unrelated third parties. Information may be shared only when required to operate the tool, comply with law, protect security, or communicate with TikTok services at the operator's request.

Storage and Security

Authentication data and operational data are intended to be stored only in access-controlled systems. Reasonable technical and organizational safeguards are used to reduce unauthorized access, disclosure, or loss. No internet-connected system can be guaranteed perfectly secure.

Retention

Information is kept only as long as needed for the purposes described above, including operation, debugging, audit history, backup, security, or legal compliance. Temporary media and logs may be deleted when they are no longer needed.

User Control and Deletion

If TikTok account access has been granted, access can be revoked through TikTok account settings or by requesting deletion of stored TikTok-related data. After revocation, the tool will no longer be able to access new TikTok data unless authorization is granted again.

Children's Privacy

This tool is not directed to children and is not intended to knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.

Contact

For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact the operator through the GitHub repository that hosts this policy.
