# Topic Brief — Car Payment Trap

Topic: How long car loans and monthly-payment shopping turn transportation into a paycheck trap for younger households.

Core angle: Car buyers are trained to ask whether the monthly payment fits, while the real trap is the full loan cost, long term, interest, insurance, negative equity, and the way one car decision can lock up years of cash flow.

Stat bomb: Experian reports average monthly payments around the mid-$700s for new vehicles and low/mid-$500s for used vehicles in 2025 context; New York Fed household debt reports put auto-loan balances around the $1.6T scale and track elevated delinquency pressure.

Laura/John tension: John remembers cars as expensive but temporary tools; Laura faces a market where monthly payments, insurance, repairs, loan terms, and negative equity can turn a basic car into a long-term paycheck claim.
