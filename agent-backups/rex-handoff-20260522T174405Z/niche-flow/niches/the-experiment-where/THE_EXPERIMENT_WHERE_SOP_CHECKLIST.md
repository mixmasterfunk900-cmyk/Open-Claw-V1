# The Experiment Where — SOP Checklist

Use only the SOP files in `niches/the-experiment-where/sop/`.

Do not use Dog Psychology SOPs for this niche.

## Script gate

- Title follows: `The Experiment Where [subject] [past-tense verb/action]`.
- Target length: 2,000–2,600 words. Masala override for this niche; do not use the shorter draft target.
- Result is stated inside the first 45 seconds.
- Hook uses Historical Act + One Rule + Forbidden Question unless result-first is explicitly better.
- Structure follows eight blocks:
  1. Inciting act
  2. The figure
  3. Results + first thesis
  4. Modern parallel 1
  5. Modern parallel 2
  6. The mechanism
  7. Edge case
  8. Conclusion
- At least two modern parallels/case studies.
- Names researcher, year, and institution for modern parallels.
- No mid-roll CTA.
- Final line is one sentence, a paradox or inversion.
- Avoid forbidden words: fascinating, incredible, amazing, interestingly, surprisingly, notably, crucially, importantly.
- No moralizing/editorializing.
- No vague sourcing such as “studies show”.

## Visual gate

- Sparse melancholic 2D hand-drawn doodle style.
- Hollow dark-circled eyes as signature.
- Muted palette only.
- Wide negative space.
- Minimal environments.
- No bright colours, no cheerful cartoon style, no logos/watermarks.
- Contact-sheet QA must show coherent story progression.

## Thumbnail gate

- Default template: The Hollow Subject, unless authority figure is central.
- One focal subject or authority-vs-subject composition.
- 5 words maximum, or no text.
- No bright colours, no red/yellow accents.
