## Description
The monthly payment is not the price.

In this episode of Laura & John’s Money Gap, John and Laura break down the car payment trap: how dealerships and lenders can make an expensive car feel affordable by focusing on one clean monthly number instead of the total cost of ownership.

We cover:
- why monthly-payment framing works so well
- how long loan terms can hide the real cost
- why insurance, repairs, fuel, fees, and interest matter
- how negative equity turns a car into a debt container
- warning signs before signing a car loan
- seven questions to ask before financing a vehicle

A car can be a necessary tool. The goal is not to shame anyone for needing transportation. The goal is to make the full cost visible before a payment quietly takes over your paycheck.
