## Automated QA Gate

Before a frame can be stitched into the preview, inspect it against this checklist:

- Script match: the frame must directly show the narration beat for its timestamp.
- Anatomy: human has exactly two hands total; no duplicate hands, extra fingers, broken wrists, or merged limbs.
- Dog anatomy: one dog only, four legs maximum, no extra paws, no warped muzzle, no duplicated ears.
- Contact logic: if the script mentions a paw on wrist, knee, or arm, the paw must touch that exact body part.
- Originality: no copied compositions from supplied style references; references are for polish, palette discipline, and line quality only.
- Palette: owner clothing and emotional accents should stay in dusty coral, terracotta, amber, or warm rose; avoid dominant sage/olive clothing.
- Clutter: no large symbolic diagram unless the narration calls for it.
- Text: no readable generated words or numbers inside the image.
- Reject/regenerate if any item fails. Do not stitch unapproved frames.
