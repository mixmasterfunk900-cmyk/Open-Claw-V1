## Selected posts

### 1. Hot take and thesis

Building in public is not posting every win.

It is letting people watch the messy middle without turning it into a performance.

The bugs, the bad thumbnails, the agent loop that barely works, that is where trust gets built.

### 2. Failure and fix receipt

The best build in public posts usually start with:

“I thought this part would be easy.”

That sentence has carried half my project.

Every broken render, failed upload flow, and weird agent mistake became better content than a polished launch thread.

### 3. Useful contrast

Building in public works when people can see the receipts.

Not vague momentum.
Not “big things coming.”

Show the broken version, the fix, the next bottleneck, and what changed because of it.
